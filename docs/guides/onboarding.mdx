## After Registration

When you register for the first time, AI Firewall shows an onboarding screen with three options. All three create the same underlying data structure — the only difference is the guided setup steps shown afterward.

## Setup Options

### Individual
**"Just me, working solo"**

Creates a personal organization with you as the admin. No teams, no members to manage. You get:
- Full security scanning on every AI request
- All LLM providers available
- Personal file restrictions
- Usage tracking

### Team
**"A small team sharing one workspace"**

Creates an organization and prompts you to name it and invite team members. You get everything in Individual plus:
- Invite team members with different roles
- Shared security policies
- Per-member usage analytics

### Organization
**"Multiple teams with separate policies"**

Creates an organization and guides you through creating teams within it. You get everything in Team plus:
- Multiple teams with team leads
- Per-team file restrictions
- Per-team policy overrides
- Role-based access control

## Naming Your Workspace

After choosing, you'll set:
- **Name** — human-readable (e.g., "Acme Corp" or "My Workspace")
- **URL slug** — used in URLs (e.g., `firewall/acme-corp`), lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only

## Upgrading Later

You can always scale up without migrating data:

| From | To | How |
|------|----|-----|
| Individual | Team | Go to Team page → invite members |
| Individual | Organization | Go to Team page → create teams → invite members |
| Team | Organization | Go to Team page → create additional teams with different policies |

Your organization already exists. Teams and members are additive — just add them when you're ready.
